RCLONE privacy policy

This policy describes the private Google Drive synchronization setup operated by moneypro.

Data accessed and purpose

With account authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata, including names, folder structure, identifiers, sizes, and modification times. It uses this access to synchronize files between authorized Google Drive locations and storage managed by the operator. Synchronization may create, update, or delete files.

Storage and access

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the operator's rclone configuration. Synchronized files, synchronization state, and diagnostic logs are stored on operator-managed systems. Logs may include file names and error details. Google processes data through its Drive and authentication services. Existing permissions on synchronized Google Drive locations continue to apply.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used to provide and maintain the configured synchronization. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and revocation

Local copies, logs, synchronization state, and credentials remain until removed by the operator or the configured retention process. Users can revoke the application's access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously synchronized files or logs. Contact the operator to request removal of locally retained data.

Website and contact

These informational pages contain no application analytics or tracking scripts. The website hosting provider may process standard request information to serve the pages. Contact the operator through the moneypro GitHub profile.
