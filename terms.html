These terms describe the private Google Drive synchronization setup operated by moneypro. They apply to this setup, not to the separate open-source rclone project.

Authorized use

This setup is for the operator and users permitted by the operator. It is not a public sign-up service. Only authorize accounts and synchronize files that you own or have permission to access and manage. Use must comply with applicable laws and the terms of the connected services.

Synchronization

Rclone synchronizes files between authorized Google Drive locations and operator-managed storage. Depending on the configuration, it may create, download, upload, update, or delete files. Review the intended synchronization locations with the operator before authorizing access and maintain independent backups of important files.

Availability

This private setup is provided on an as-available basis. Synchronization can be interrupted by connectivity, authentication, service limits, or software errors. Continuous availability and successful synchronization of every change are not guaranteed.

Privacy and ending access

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. You can stop future authorized Google API access by revoking this application's permission in Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically remove existing synchronized copies or logs; contact the operator about their removal.

Contact

For questions about this setup or to request that synchronization stop, contact the operator through the moneypro GitHub profile.
